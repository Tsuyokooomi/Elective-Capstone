# Laptop Loan or Replacement Request
SOP ID: KB-015
Category: Devices and Software
Applies to: Employees whose laptop is broken, lost, or too old to use.
Action: create_ticket
Escalation: If the laptop was lost or stolen (see KB-017).

Loan laptop: IT gives a loan laptop while yours is in repair. A loan lasts up to 5 business days.

Replacement: A replacement is possible when the laptop is 4 or more years old, or when repair is not possible.

Steps:
1. Back up your files to the company cloud drive if the laptop still turns on.
2. Submit a ticket with the asset tag (the label on the laptop), a description of the fault, and your manager's approval.
3. IT confirms the request and tells you when to collect the device.
4. Collect the laptop at the IT desk, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
5. Return the loan laptop within 2 business days after your own laptop is back.

Escalate to a human if: the request is urgent, or the laptop was lost or stolen.

Related SOPs: KB-011, KB-017
