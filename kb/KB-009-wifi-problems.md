# Wi-Fi Connection Problems
SOP ID: KB-009
Category: Network and Email
Applies to: Employees and visitors in CapstoneCompany offices.
Action: None
Escalation: If many people are affected.

Networks:
- CapstoneCompany-Corp: for employees. Sign in with your work account.
- CapstoneCompany-Guest: for visitors only. Employees must not use it.

Steps:
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again.
2. Select "Forget" for CapstoneCompany-Corp, then reconnect and sign in again.
3. If you changed your password recently, sign in with the new one.
4. Restart your laptop.
5. Move closer to an access point and check whether other people have the same problem.

Escalate to a human if: several people in the same area lose Wi-Fi at once. This is a possible outage and counts as a P2 ticket (see KB-019).
