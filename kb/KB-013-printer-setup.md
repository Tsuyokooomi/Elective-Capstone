# Printer Setup and Troubleshooting
SOP ID: KB-013
Category: Devices and Software
Applies to: Office printers at CapstoneCompany.
Action: None
Escalation: For hardware faults.

Add a printer:
1. Open Settings, then Bluetooth and devices, then Printers and scanners.
2. Select Add device.
3. Pick the printer by its location code, which is on a label on the printer (for example, 3F-North).
4. Print a test page.

If a job does not print:
1. Check that the printer is on and shows no error.
2. Check paper and toner.
3. Open the print queue and cancel stuck jobs.
4. Print again.
5. Restart the printer.
6. Remove the printer and add it again.

Notes:
- You must be on the office network or the VPN to print.

Escalate to a human if: the printer shows a hardware error, paper jams repeat, or toner needs replacement.
