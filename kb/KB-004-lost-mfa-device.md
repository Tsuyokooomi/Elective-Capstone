# Lost or Replaced MFA Device
SOP ID: KB-004
Category: Accounts and Access
Applies to: Employees who lost, broke, or replaced the phone that holds their MFA app.
Action: escalate_to_human
Escalation: Always. The assistant cannot reset MFA.

Why a human handles this: MFA protects against account takeover. A person must confirm your identity before a new device is added.

Steps for the employee:
1. If you saved backup codes (see KB-003), use one to sign in and add your new device.
2. If you have no backup code, contact the helpdesk and give your employee ID.
3. A helpdesk agent confirms your identity. The agent may ask your manager to confirm by email or phone.
4. The agent removes the old device and sends you a time-limited enrolment link.
5. Set up the new device with that link (see KB-003).

Notes:
- Expect the process to take up to one business day.
- If the old phone was lost or stolen, also follow KB-017.

Related SOPs: KB-003, KB-017
