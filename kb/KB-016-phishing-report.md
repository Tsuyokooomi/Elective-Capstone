# Reporting a Phishing Email
SOP ID: KB-016
Category: Security
Applies to: Any suspicious email, text message, or link.
Action: escalate_to_human
Escalation: Always. The security team reviews every report.

Signs of phishing: urgent threats, requests for passwords or codes, unknown senders, strange links, attachments you did not expect.

Steps:
1. Do not click links, open attachments, or reply.
2. In Outlook, select Report, then Phishing. If you do not see the button, forward the email as an attachment to security@capstonecompany.example.
3. Delete the email after you report it.

If you already clicked or entered a password:
1. Reset your password at once (see KB-001).
2. Contact the helpdesk now and say you may have been phished. This is a P1 case (see KB-019).
3. Do not turn off your computer. Disconnect it from the network if the security team asks.

Related SOPs: KB-001, KB-018, KB-019
