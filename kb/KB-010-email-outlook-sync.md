# Email and Outlook Sync Problems
SOP ID: KB-010
Category: Network and Email
Applies to: Employees whose Outlook does not send, receive, or update mail.
Action: None
Escalation: If webmail also fails.

Steps:
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Look at the bottom of Outlook. If it says "Working Offline", select Send/Receive, then turn off Work Offline.
3. Restart Outlook.
4. Open webmail at mail.capstonecompany.example. If mail works there, the problem is in Outlook.
5. Check your mailbox size. The limit is 50 GB. Delete or archive old mail if you are near it.
6. Remove your account from Outlook and add it again.
7. Install pending Windows and Office updates.

Notes:
- If you changed your password recently, Outlook may need you to sign in again.

Escalate to a human if: webmail also fails, or you cannot sign in even with the correct password.
