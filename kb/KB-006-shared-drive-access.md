# Shared Drive or Folder Access Request
SOP ID: KB-006
Category: Accounts and Access
Applies to: Employees who need access to a shared drive or folder.
Action: create_ticket
Escalation: For restricted folders.

Steps:
1. Find the full folder path, for example `\\fileserver\Marketing\Campaigns`.
2. Ask the folder owner to approve your access. The owner is usually the team lead.
3. Submit a ticket with the folder path, the access level (read or edit), the owner's name, and the reason.
4. IT applies the access within 1 business day.
5. Sign out and sign in again, or restart your laptop, so the new access takes effect.

Notes:
- Request read access unless you must change files.
- Access is reviewed every 6 months.

Escalate to a human if: the folder is marked Restricted, such as HR or Finance folders. These need approval from the data owner and the security team.
