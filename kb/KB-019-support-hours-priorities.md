# IT Support Hours and Ticket Priorities
SOP ID: KB-019
Category: Policy
Applies to: All CapstoneCompany employees who use IT support.
Action: None
Escalation: Not applicable.

Support hours:
- Human helpdesk: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Philippine Time, except company holidays.
- Helpdesk assistant (chat): available 24 hours for first-level questions.
- Emergency hotline (ext. 100): 24 hours, for P1 cases only.

Ticket priorities:
- P1 Critical: security incident, company-wide outage. First response in 15 minutes. Target fix in 4 hours.
- P2 High: you cannot work and have no workaround, or a group is affected. First response in 1 hour. Target fix in 8 hours.
- P3 Medium: a problem with a workaround. First response in 4 hours. Target fix in 3 business days.
- P4 Low: requests and questions. First response in 1 business day. Target fix in 5 business days.

Tips:
- Include the error message, the time, and what you tried. A full ticket gets fixed faster.
- Do not open duplicate tickets. Reply to the existing one.
