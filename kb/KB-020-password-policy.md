# Password Policy
SOP ID: KB-020
Category: Policy
Applies to: All CapstoneCompany accounts.
Action: None
Escalation: Not applicable.

Requirements:
- At least 12 characters.
- Use at least three of these four types: uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, symbols.
- Do not use your name, your email address, or the company name.
- Do not reuse any of your last 10 passwords.
- Change your password every 180 days. The system warns you 14 days before.

Rules:
- Never share your password with anyone. IT staff never ask for it.
- Use a different password for each account. Do not reuse your work password on personal sites.
- Use the approved company password manager to store passwords.
- Never write a password in chat, email, or a sticky note.

Lockout: 5 failed attempts lock the account for 30 minutes (see KB-002).
