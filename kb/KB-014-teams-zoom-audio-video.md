# Teams or Zoom Audio and Video Problems
SOP ID: KB-014
Category: Devices and Software
Applies to: Microphone, speaker, or camera problems in Teams or Zoom.
Action: None
Escalation: If the device is not detected.

Steps:
1. In the app, open Settings, then Devices. Pick the correct microphone, speaker, and camera.
2. Check that your headset is not muted and that the volume is up.
3. Open Windows Settings, then Privacy and security. Make sure Microphone and Camera access is on for the app.
4. Close other apps that may use the camera or microphone.
5. Unplug the headset or camera, wait 10 seconds, and plug it in again.
6. Update Teams or Zoom to the latest version.
7. Test the device in another app, such as the Windows Camera app.

Escalate to a human if: Windows does not list the device in Device Manager, or it fails in every app. This may be a hardware fault.
