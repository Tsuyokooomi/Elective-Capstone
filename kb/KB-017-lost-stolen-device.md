# Lost or Stolen Device
SOP ID: KB-017
Category: Security
Applies to: Any CapstoneCompany laptop, phone, or tablet, and any personal phone that holds your MFA app.
Action: create_ticket (P1) and escalate_to_human
Escalation: Always, and at once.

Report the loss right away, even if you are not sure it is gone. Speed protects company data.

Steps:
1. Call the helpdesk hotline at ext. 100 (open 24 hours for P1 cases) and tell the security team.
2. Give the device type, the asset tag if you have it, the time and place you last had it, and whether it was locked.
3. IT locks your account sessions and sends a remote lock or wipe command to the device.
4. Reset your password from another device (see KB-001).
5. If the device was stolen, file a police report and send the report number to the security team.
6. If the device held your MFA app, follow KB-004 to enrol a new one.
7. Request a replacement or loan laptop (see KB-015).

Notes:
- You will not be blamed for reporting early.

Related SOPs: KB-001, KB-004, KB-015, KB-019
