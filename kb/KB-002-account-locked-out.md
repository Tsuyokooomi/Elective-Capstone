# Account Locked Out
SOP ID: KB-002
Category: Accounts and Access
Applies to: All CapstoneCompany employees.
Action: None (the user can fix it with KB-001)
Escalation: If lockouts repeat.

What happens: After 5 failed sign-in attempts, your account locks for 30 minutes. It unlocks on its own after that time.

Steps:
1. Wait 30 minutes and try again, or reset your password now with KB-001. A reset unlocks the account at once.
2. Check that Caps Lock is off and that you use your work email as the username.
3. Look for old saved passwords. A phone, a tablet, or a second laptop with the old password causes repeat lockouts. Update or remove it.
4. Sign in again.

Escalate to a human if: the account locks more than twice in one day, or you did not try to sign in at the times of the failed attempts. This may be an attack, and the security team must review it.
